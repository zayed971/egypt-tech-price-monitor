# Egypt import rules for tech (research, read-only web, 2026-10-07)

Evidence labels: OFFICIAL = government, carrier or manufacturer page. SECONDARY = news, law firm or aggregator. WEAK = aggregator that admits it is unverified.
Exa hit its free rate limit mid-run, so some items rest on search snippets only; those are marked "snippet".

## A. Egyptian customs: passengers

- Phones (OFFICIAL, NTRA, 2026-01-20): "the Egyptian Customs Authority and the National Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Egypt (NTRA) announce the annulment of the exceptional exemption granted for the imported mobile phone carried by a passenger, effective 12:00 noon on Wednesday, January 21, 2026. The exemption will continue to apply to mobile phones of Egyptians living abroad and to tourists for a period of 90 days."
  https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/end-of-exceptional-exemption-on-imported-mobile-phones-carried-by-passenger-following-success-of-local-manufacturing-at-competitive-prices-effective-1200-noon-wednesday-january-21-2026/
- Phone payment channel (same NTRA page): "taxes and fees imposed on mobile phones imported from abroad can be paid through 'Telephony' app., in addition to the digital payment channels available via banks and e-wallets. A 90-day grace period, effective the date mobile phone was activated, will be granted". Also: "the registration of personal mobile phones at customs offices is canceled".
- Phone fee size (SECONDARY, The National, 2026-01-21): "any mobile device entering the country with a traveller will be subject to a one-time 38.5 per cent charge of the device's cost covering customs and taxes." https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/2026/01/21/egypts-phone-import-tax-branded-unfair-as-grace-period-comes-to-an-end/
  CONFLICT: EnterpriseAM (2026-01-21) says "equivalent to 37.5% of the customs value of the device"; Ahram (2025-01-11) says 38.5 percent. https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/01/21/no-more-exempting-imported-mobile-phones-from-customs/ . Use 38.5% (two sources) but no official page states the percentage. Unpaid phones: "devices that have not been cleared will be deactivated across all Egyptian networks" (Ecofin, 2026-01-21, snippet).
- Customs Law 207/2020, Art. 20 (OFFICIAL text, date not shown): "The customs tax shall be exempted on the following ... 2- Personal effects of passengers whether they were tourists, transiting or residences in the country upon arrival or departing." http://www.acs.org.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Customs-Law-207-2020-.pdf
- Personal-use test (OFFICIAL, Arabic, customs.gov.eg, date not shown). My translation of the conditions: items arrive in the name of a natural person; are needed by the person or family; do "not arrive in quantities that carry a trade character"; "for devices, they must be of household-use types"; customs decides personal use by "social standing and number of visits through the customs outlet"; quantities above personal use are held. https://www.customs.gov.eg/Legislations/TravelAndTourism?travelAndTourismId=2
- New-goods value exemption (SECONDARY, EnterpriseAM, 2023-02-07): "Tourists and citizens entering the country will now be able to bring up to EGP 15k worth of new purchases for personal use or as gifts, up from EGP 10k previously." https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2023/02/07/higher-personal-exemption-limits-new-taxes-on-dutyfree-alcohol/ (Egypt Independent 2023-02-02: "exempted from customs up to LE15,000").
  CONFLICT: EntryBrief (reviewed 2026-10-01) still says "Personal goods and gifts up to EGP 10,000 ... are duty-free"; GoodsAcrossBorders says EGP 10,000 and admits its figures "have not been re-checked against the live page" (WEAK). No 2025-2026 official change to EGP 15,000 was found. Treat EGP 15,000 as best-supported but unconfirmed for 2026.
- Per person: MoF Customs Procedures Manual (COP27 variant, 2022): "If one family member or all family members are eligible for exemptions stated herein, the exemption limit shall be granted separately to each one of them." https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/2022-09/654584b0-351a-11ed-a96d-e13e135fe019.pdf (conference-specific, applying it to normal travel is an inference).
- Declaration duty (same MoF manual): "Other items must be also declared if they exceed the permitted limit allowed for personal use ... Passengers failing to declare the items they are carrying are held responsible and accountable under the customs law".
- Tourist rule (Qatar embassy Cairo page restating Egyptian rules, date not shown): "all carried items including cameras, audio and video recorders ... mobile phones ... provided that they are not meant for trade." https://cairo.embassy.qa/en/egypt/travel-tips . This is the tourist rule, not necessarily the rule for an Egyptian arriving.
- Cash (SECONDARY, EgyptToday, 2025-08-13): "Current customs regulations allow individuals to carry up to $10,000 or its equivalent without a declaration ... travelers may take up to LE 5,000 in local currency when entering or exiting the country." https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/141676/Travelers-gain-easier-access-to-foreign-currency-as-banks-relax
- NOT CONFIRMED: any official rule giving a number of laptops or tablets per passenger; any rule on new vs used laptops; any rule specific to GPU, SSD, RAM carried by a passenger. The only test found is "personal use, no trade quantities, EGP value exemption". Several GPUs or many SSDs/RAM kits risk being treated as trade quantities (inference from the test above). Decree 430/2021 (Consortio translation, 2021-08-31) contains "The items imported must be personal-use articles that are not new, other than personal computers and their accessories" for a different exemption article; context unclear. https://consortiolawfirm.com/egyptian-customs-law-executive-regulations-ministerial-decree-430-2021-english-translation/
- 2025-2026 changes found: phone exemption ended 2026-01-21 (above). MoF Decision 548/2025 amended the Executive Regulation (PwC, March 2026): "ACID controls now restrict tariff amendments and enforce a six-month validity". Nothing on passenger allowances. https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/tax/documents/2026/amendments-egypt-customs-law.pdf

## B. Courier and post into Egypt

- Duty and VAT range (OFFICIAL, DHL country guide, date not shown): "Import duty varies from 0% to 60% according to the classification of goods. Tax can vary from 0% to 14%, and this is added to the sum of customs value and duties." Also "all international shipments are subject to blanket inspection". https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-global/e-commerce-advice/shipping-guides-by-country/country-guide-egypt
- Laptop duty (SECONDARY): hscodedb for HS 8471.30: "the representative MFN general duty rate is 0%", VAT "14.00 %", base "CIF + Duty" (page says tariff data verified July 2026). https://hscodedb.com/eg/hs-code/8471-30/ . GroWrk (date not shown): "Laptops typically carry a 0% customs duty, but are subject to 14% VAT. Plus, 1-3% additional fees". https://growrk.com/country-guide-egypt . FGX: "Import Tax 14%", "Import Duty Rate for Laptops 0%". https://fgx.com/importer-of-record/egypt
- NOT FOUND: official tariff lines for SSD, RAM, GPU, monitors, peripherals, mini PCs. An aggregator claims "5-20%" for IT gear (GroWrk), unverified. No "table tax" or other surcharge on laptops found.
- Low-value threshold: NOT CONFIRMED officially. Aggregators claim EGP 1,000 (hscodedb) or about US$40 (GoodsAcrossBorders, "As of: 2023-01-01", WEAK). Irrelevant for laptops and components, which exceed it.
- DHL clearance fees (OFFICIAL, DHL Egypt, effective 2024-11-02): "Duty Tax Processing: 5 USD or 2% of the fiscal charge, if higher"; "Release to Broker: 13 USD per Shipment"; "Bonded Transit: 11 USD per Shipment"; "Permits and Licenses: 6.60 USD per Shipment"; "Non-Routine Entry: 13 USD per Shipment"; "Bonded Storage: 1 USD per Shipment per Day Plus 0.12 per KG per Day after 3 calendar Days". "In case the sender has not settled duties and taxes for the shipment, the recipient is required to pay the DHL Express Customs fees." Undeliverable shipments "could be returned to the country of origin within the legal period (15 days from the arrival date)". https://www.dhl.com/eg-en/home/important-information/2024/04082024.html
- FedEx, Aramex, Egypt Post fee tables and clearance times: NOT FOUND.
- ACI for air (SECONDARY, AHK Egypt, date not shown): "the ACI system will become mandatory for all air freight shipments starting January 1, 2026." Courier carve-out: "courier shipments (e.g., DHL, FedEx, UPS), they are exempted from the ACI system as long as the shipment value does not exceed USD 2,000 and the total weight is under 50 kg". The same page later says the deciding factor is weight (50 kg), so it contradicts itself on the USD 2,000 limit. https://aegypten.ahk.de/en/services/aci-cargox-informations/mandatory-aci-system-for-air-freight
- Personal shipments (OFFICIAL, Nafeza ACI FAQ, date not shown): "Personal shipments have no exceptions ... The Egyptian freight forwarder ... getting the ACID through registering the Importer and his ID number (For Egyptians) or the passport number (Foreigners)". That FAQ says "The ACI system is implemented on marine imports only", so it predates the 2026 air rule. https://www.nafeza.gov.eg/web/m/docs-af091d8b410cfab6e59c5bd4a4ee04b32e6703fb968a65564f036fa8.pdf
- Consumer docs (DHL guide): "Imports for personal use purposes require ... AWB, Commercial invoice are the only documents required to clear". B2B shipments since 2023-07-01 need product registration with the Tax Authority (DHL notice). Whether an individual needs a tax ID for express courier: NOT confirmed (GroWrk lists "National ID (for individuals)", undated).
- Transit time (GroWrk, date not shown): "Express shipping (3-7 days) ... Standard shipping (7-14 days)". Egypt Post times: not found.
- Phones by courier: not found whether the phone charge is collected by the courier or through the Telephony app. Assume the app.
- Decision 548/2025 introduced "New service fees ... for customs documentation, user registration, and licensing" (PwC, March 2026); amounts not captured.

## C. UAE and Saudi side

- UAE VAT (OFFICIAL, u.ae, page dated 30 Mar 2026): "Federal Tax Authority (FTA) introduced Value Added Tax (VAT) of 5 per cent in January 2018". Refund terms: "valid on a minimum expenditure of AED 250"; "The tourist must validate the transaction at the airport within 90 days of the purchase date"; not refundable: "goods that are not in the possession of the tourist when leaving the country"; "The tourist will receive 85 per cent of the total VAT amount paid, after the deduction of a fee of AED 4.80 per tax-free tag."; "Planet is the exclusive operator". https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation/vat/tax-refund-for-tourists
  Implication (my reading): the scheme is for tourists; a UAE-resident relative is NOT shown to qualify. The page's residency wording was not captured. Sealed-bag or inspection rules for electronics: NOT confirmed (a PropertyFinder snippet says goods may need inspection before check-in).
- UAE export documents for carrying personal electronics: NOT FOUND.
- Saudi VAT 15% (SECONDARY, vatcalc.com, 2025-09-06): "In July 2020, the government tripled the rate to 15%". Refund scheme: "The traveler must be a non-resident tourist or GCC national, aged 18 or older"; "The minimum total purchase must exceed SAR 500"; "exported within 90 days"; counters at Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam airports; "cash cap of SAR 5,000 per person per day". https://www.vatcalc.com/saudi-arabia/saudi-arabia-vat-refund-scheme/ . Gulf News and Khaleej Times (snippets) say it took effect April 18 (2025) with Global Blue. Official ZATCA page not read.

## E. Warranty

- ASUS (OFFICIAL, warranty card V4, file dated 2025-09-10): "If you purchase an ASUS product in a country/region covered by ASUS's International Warranty Service and seek service in another covered country/region, ASUS will provide international warranty service and technical support."; "may require longer repair times depending on local parts availability"; "If the country/region where the repair is needed is not covered by the international warranty, repair services cannot be provided." A search summary said Egypt is on the Middle East and Africa list; the list itself was not read, so VERIFY. This card is for notebooks. https://www.asus.com/support/images/upload/file/20250910085250209_Q26123_NB_International_warranty_country_cover_list_Warranty_Card_V4_WEB.pdf
- Gigabyte (OFFICIAL laptop page, date not shown): warranty "applies to brand new Products sold by authorized resellers"; "Purchasers must first complete product registration of global warranty service." A snippet of regional text says cross-region service follows local coverage and a unit may need to return to the country of purchase. Egypt coverage NOT confirmed. https://www.gigabyte.com/Support/Consumer/Warranty/Laptop
- Lenovo (snippet of official page): "International Warranty Services (IWS) enables customers who travel with or relocate any IWS eligible Lenovo product to receive warranty service in any country where that product is sold and serviced." Egypt eligibility NOT confirmed. https://support.lenovo.com/be/en/warranty-upgrade-and-services
- HP (hp.com/gb-en limited warranty page, date not shown): the fetched text did not show the transfer clause. Snippet summary, not verbatim: products "may be transferred to another country/region, where HP or its authorized service providers offer warranty service for the same product model number". https://www.hp.com/gb-en/privacy/limited_warranty.html
- Dell: only forum snippets (the basic 12 month depot warranty "doesn't transfer"); official statement NOT found.
- Logitech (OFFICIAL, date not shown): "Your product's warranty is only valid in the country in which you purchased your product. Logitech does not offer International Warranty." https://support.logi.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360023183334
- WD (OFFICIAL, date not shown): "No warranty service is provided unless the Product is returned to an authorized return center in the region (warranty regions are identified as Canada, U.S.A., Latin America, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific, India and Thailand) where the Product was first shipped by WD". A UAE or KSA purchase is probably the same Middle East/Africa region as Egypt (inference, verify). US purchase: different region. https://www.westerndigital.com/en-kw/support/store/warranty-policy/wd
- Kingston (OFFICIAL, Arabic page, date not shown): proof of purchase must show the original purchase date and place (translated); the visible text has no country restriction. Egypt service route NOT confirmed. https://www.kingston.com/unitedkingdom/en/wa/warranty_sa
- Samsung: only an Ireland shop snippet ("when you buy a non-European device, it is not covered under European Warranty Policy"); Samsung Egypt terms NOT found.
- Apple: only forum/news snippets (iPhone service may be limited to the country of sale; Mac regional parts swapped). No official Apple page read. NOT confirmed.
- MSI, Crucial: NOT found.
- Practical read: only ASUS and probably Lenovo and HP advertise cross-country service; Logitech says no; WD is regional. Treat all others as "local warranty not guaranteed" and get written confirmation from the Egyptian distributor before buying.

## F. Payments and FX

- Markup fee (SECONDARY, EgyptToday, 2025-08-13): "Both NBE and CIB have reduced these charges from 5 percent to 3 percent, with Bank Misr introducing a similar cut effective August 13, 2025." The charges are the foreign currency markup on credit card transactions. https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/141676/Travelers-gain-easier-access-to-foreign-currency-as-banks-relax
- Limits, same article: "The AAIB has also expanded its international card spending cap to $10,000". A CairoScene headline (2026-03-10) says banks tightened foreign-currency card limits and a snippet gave CIB "EGP 15,000 monthly for basic cards and EGP 100,000 for platinum" and NBE up to "100,000 Egyptian pounds per month". The CairoScene fetch returned only a navigation page, so this is UNVERIFIED. Current CIB, NBE, Banque Misr limits for 2026: NOT confirmed. https://cairoscene.com/buzz/egypt-s-banks-tighten-limits-for-foreign-currency-credit-card-spending
- ATM abroad (snippet from one bank's fee schedule, bank unnamed): "fees for withdrawing cash from ATMs outside Egypt at 10% with a minimum 100 EGP".
- PayPal (OFFICIAL, PayPal EG, date not shown): "To receive payments with PayPal, choose a Visa card issued in Egypt or a bank account in the U.S. to link to your PayPal account. You must withdraw payments from your available balance before you can spend it." This page covers receiving and withdrawing. Whether an Egyptian card can fund PayPal purchases: NOT confirmed. https://www.paypal.com/eg/webapps/mpp/setup-to-receive-withdraw
- Typical total fee: only the 3% bank markup is sourced. The bank's FX spread and any foreign-transaction fee were not found, so treat 3% as a floor and the all-in figure as unconfirmed.
