## What I would choose

- Mostly desk work, existing monitor/keyboard: investigate the 44–46k desktop towers with 32GB RAM and larger SSD. For scenes that need more GPU memory, consider the 5060 Ti 16GB tower if the total after RAM and PSU changes fits your budget.
- Need portability or an included display: the 54,499 EGP LOQ RTX 4060 remains the value laptop candidate. It has 12GB RAM and its Ryzen 7 7435HS lacks integrated graphics, limiting battery efficiency. The 69,499 EGP i7-14700HX/5060 LOQ is a CPU-focused alternative, but leaves almost no room for upgrades and ships with DOS.
- CPU-heavy versus GPU-heavy work should guide the comparison. The 7600X tower has fewer CPU cores than the i7-14700HX laptop; 16GB GPU memory alone does not make it faster at every task. Desktop cooling, replaceable components and future upgrades are practical advantages. No matched Blender/Rhino measurements were obtained, so no percentage speed advantage is claimed.

Check the exact PSU model and component substitutions: the Softlink page says Xigmatek 600W without a precise PSU model; Elyamama lists Antec Atom V650; Compumarts lists a Xander case/750W Bronze supply. Efficiency labels alone do not establish PSU quality.

## Import comparison

No destination-specific Amazon US checkout was verified. At the recorded exchange-rate baseline of 52.24751 EGP/USD and an illustrative 3% card fee, a comparable overseas purchase must have its item + shipping + import charges below approximately **$1,013** to beat the 54,499 EGP local laptop, or **$1,291** to beat the 69,499 EGP local laptop. Your actual bank rate/fee may differ. These are break-even calculations, not live overseas offers.

Buying locally avoids overseas return shipping, but the exact local warranty type remains a retailer-specific question. An overseas price becomes a buying candidate only after configuration, delivery eligibility, total landed price and warranty arrangements are confirmed.
