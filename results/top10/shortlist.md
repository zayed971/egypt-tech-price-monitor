7. **EVGA RTX 3060 XC 12GB:** Low-cost 12GB NVIDIA GPU candidate for an existing or custom PC. Confirm new/used/refurbished condition, warranty, PSU and physical fit.
8. **Lexar THOR 32GB DDR4-3200 (2x16GB):** Matched desktop RAM kit for compatible DDR4 systems. Not DDR5 and not laptop SO-DIMM; confirm motherboard support and XMP settings.
9. **Acer SD200 16GB DDR5-5600 SO-DIMM:** Verified single-module laptop memory option. Confirm DDR5 SO-DIMM support, available slots and supported capacity; installed speed may be lower.
10. **KLEVV CRAS C715 1TB NVMe Gen3:** Lowest collected eligible new internal 1TB NVMe candidate in this review. Confirm M.2 2280 NVMe fit; delivery and installation extra. No DRAM-cache claim.
